## Construction notes

- **15 holdings, weights 4–10%,** chosen from the 233 companies in batches 1–9 that passed both layers. A company is shortlisted if it has an operating margin of at least 20%, ROIC of at least 15%, debt/equity below 1.5, and a forward P/E judged fair or low against its own 5-year median. From that shortlist, the picks were spread across sectors. Weights start equal (~6.7%) and are tilted up for the highest quality at a reasonable price (TSM, ASML, V). They are tilted down for rich valuations (AAPL), forecast uncertainty (ADBE), or a holding's role as a diversifier (EOG, AEM).
- **Sector mix:** Technology is 29% (semiconductors 19%, software 5%, hardware 5%). Healthcare is 27%, materials 11% (industrial gases and gold), industrials 12%, payments 9%, consumer staples 7% and energy 5%. **There are no banks, insurers or most conventional financials**, because they fail the Shariah screens. **XOM, CVX and VLO were excluded on ethics grounds**; EOG (no findings) is the energy holding. **Many large US tech names are absent** because they fail Shariah (MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META) or ethics (NVDA, CSCO, CRM, PANW and others).

## Concentration risks

1. **One semiconductor supply chain (~19%, 24% with Apple):** TSM and ASML depend on the same chip-capex cycle and on Taiwan. A Taiwan crisis or tighter export controls would hit them together, and Apple too.
2. **The GLP-1 obesity-drug theme (LLY + NVO = 14%):** both depend on the same drug class, pricing and US policy.
3. **Healthcare (27%)** carries drug-pricing policy risk (US most-favoured-nation pricing, IRA negotiation) across LLY, NVO, AZN and VRTX.
4. **Growth style:** most holdings trade above 20x forward earnings, so a broad market de-rating would hit the whole portfolio.
5. **Shariah status can change quarterly.** A holding that turns non-compliant would need to be sold, so check Zoya and Musaffa again before any trade.
6. **The portfolio draws only on batches 1–9 (the ~900 largest stocks).** Smaller companies have not been ethics-reviewed, so the portfolio may change as more batches are covered.

## Main risks

- Shariah statuses were scraped from public pages. Confirm each one in the Zoya and Musaffa apps.
- The ethics screen relies on AFSC, Who Profits, BDS and web searches. Absence of evidence is not proof of no involvement. Supply chains (Apple, Tesla) carry (c) forced-labour flags.
- The fundamentals come from stockanalysis.com, an aggregator, and could not all be checked against the 10-K/20-F filings. Foreign filers (NVO, TSM, ASML, AZN) report in their own currencies; the growth rates shown are in those currencies.
- Valuation calls use a simple rule: forward P/E against the company's own 5-year median P/E. That rule ignores interest rates and changes in growth outlook.
